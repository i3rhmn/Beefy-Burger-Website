A Reflection on Building the Beefy Burger Website

Summary of the Work

The Beefy Burger website project took 10 days, with our team working 5 hours per day at Bahrain Polytechnic University. Each member had specific responsibilities in building a functional and visually engaging website for a fictional burger restaurant. Abdulrahman Slais (ID: 202100782) led the project, managing JavaScript, CSS, and ensuring the team stayed on track, while also guiding others through the coding process.

Abdulrahman Altairey (ID: 202304613) handled the About Us page, focusing on showcasing the brand's values. Mohammed Al-Asfoor (ID: 202201250) designed the overall look and developed the Menu page. His design choices helped maintain visual consistency across the site.

Mohammed Sadiq (ID: 2002200511) worked on the Home page, with support from Abdulrahman Slais, ensuring the page was both navigable and inviting. Abdula Rashdan (ID: 202200568) developed the Staff page, making it personable and engaging to highlight the people behind Beefy Burger.

The remaining pages, such as the Making Of page, were handled collaboratively to ensure consistency. We held regular in-person meetings at Bahrain Polytechnic to discuss design decisions, address challenges, and support each other, resulting in a unified and functional website.

Challenges Faced and Solutions

One significant challenge was the varying coding skill levels within our group. Abdula Rashdan had limited experience with HTML and CSS, but Abdulrahman Slais provided guidance to help Abdula Rashdan improve his skills. This mentorship helped Abdula build confidence and fostered a collaborative spirit among the team.

Scheduling was also challenging due to differing commitments. We decided on Bahrain Polytechnic as our meeting place, and managed our time by setting clear goals for each session. This approach helped us stay on schedule.

Maintaining design consistency across pages was another hurdle. Mohammed Al-Asfoor led the creation of a design system and style guide, which included color palette, fonts, and layout guidelines. This ensured a cohesive and professional look across all pages, making it easier for us to work independently but achieve a unified result.

Lessons Learned

This project provided a valuable learning experience. Technically, we deepened our understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, especially in creating modular code. By developing reusable components like staff members and menu items, we ensured consistent styling and reduced redundancy, which made updates easier.

For newer team members like Abdula Rashdan, the project offered hands-on experience in structuring HTML and applying CSS styles. Collaborative learning helped Abdula grow significantly during the project.

We also learned the importance of effective communication and clear roles. Abdulrahman Slais's leadership in managing timelines and supporting teammates ensured everyone understood their responsibilities. Clear roles reduced confusion and improved the workflow.

The value of a style guide was another key lesson. Using a consistent color scheme, such as yellow for branding and neutral tones for contrast, made the site visually appealing and easy to navigate. This consistency helped us maintain a professional look and made future updates simpler.

Lastly, we learned the importance of adaptability. We had to adjust to scheduling conflicts and varying skill levels, which tested our ability to adapt. Ultimately, this helped us grow as a team, encouraging compromise and creative solutions to keep the project on track. Adaptability ensured we met deadlines and delivered a quality product.

In summary, building the Beefy Burger website taught us valuable technical skills, as well as the significance of teamwork, leadership, and adaptability. The final website reflects our combined technical efforts, creativity, and the adaptability we cultivated throughout the project.
